# 🧬 GeneProtein Intelligence (GPI)

**An AI-powered biomedical research assistant for exploring genes, proteins, structures, variants, diseases, and scientific literature.**

## 🚀 Live Application

👉 **[Open GeneProtein Intelligence](https://geneprotein-intelligence-fpt62xmhwxklhjfzfvhb9t.streamlit.app/)**

## 🔬 What is GPI?

GeneProtein Intelligence (GPI) is a research-support tool designed for students and researchers in biotechnology, biochemistry, biomedical sciences, molecular biology, genetics, bioinformatics, and related life-science fields.

GPI brings information from multiple public biomedical resources into one place and uses AI to organize and explain the retrieved evidence.

## ✨ Features

- 🧬 Gene information and identifiers
- 🔬 Protein names, functions, and annotations
- 📍 Subcellular localization
- 🧪 Disease and variant information
- 🧊 Experimental 3D protein structures from the Protein Data Bank
- 🤖 Predicted protein structures and confidence information from AlphaFold
- 📚 Scientific literature from PubMed
- 🧠 AI-assisted research synthesis using Gemini
- 🔗 Source links for further investigation
- 🔎 Natural-language searches such as "collagen 2" or "hemoglobin beta"

## 🌐 Data Sources

GPI retrieves information from established public biomedical resources, including:

- UniProt
- NCBI Gene
- PubMed
- ClinVar
- RCSB Protein Data Bank (PDB)
- AlphaFold Protein Structure Database
- PubChem

## 🎓 Who is it for?

GPI is designed especially for:

- Undergraduate students
- Postgraduate students
- Biotechnology researchers
- Biochemistry students
- Biomedical researchers
- Molecular biology and genetics students
- Bioinformatics learners

## 🛠️ Technology

- Python
- Streamlit
- Google Gemini
- REST APIs
- Public biomedical databases

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

GPI is an educational and research-support tool.

The information provided by GPI should not be considered medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or a substitute for professional medical or scientific judgment. Users should verify important information using the original scientific databases and publications.
